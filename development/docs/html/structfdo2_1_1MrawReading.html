Detailed Description

Decoded #MRAW O T S D I A P H (data sheet §4.3).

D millidegrees phase, I and A in microvolts (µV) → divide by 1000 for mV, P in microbar (µbar) → ×10⁻⁶ for mbar, H in milli-RH (mRH) → ×10⁻³ for RH.

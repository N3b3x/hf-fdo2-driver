Split exchange — send now, parse the reply later

For a caller that serves other devices while the probe measures (~150 ms): SendMeasure writes the command and returns; when the transport reports a complete line, TryReadReplyLine takes it without waiting and ParseMoxyLine / ParseMrawLine decode it. The caller owns the reply timeout. Same wire protocol as MeasureMoxy.
